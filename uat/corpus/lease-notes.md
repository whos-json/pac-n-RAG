# Harbour Point lease notes

The tenant fit-out must be approved by the landlord before works start. At the end of the lease the tenant reinstates the premises to shell condition. Rent review is upwards only.

The break notice must reach the landlord by 31 December 2026 to be valid for the June 2027 break date. The service charge cap is 4 per square foot, and the deposit is $12,000.
