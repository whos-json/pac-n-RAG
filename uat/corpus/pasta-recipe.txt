Spaghetti carbonara recipe. Ingredients: spaghetti, guanciale, egg yolks, pecorino, black pepper, salt. Serves four.

Cook the spaghetti in salted water. Fry the guanciale until crisp. Whisk the egg yolks with the pecorino and pepper. Toss the hot pasta with the guanciale off the heat, then stir in the egg mixture with a splash of pasta water until creamy. Season with salt in the kitchen, not at the table.
